# Telehealth Consultations (fictional)

## Eligibility
Telehealth is available for follow-up visits, medication reviews and test result discussions. First visits with a new specialist must be in person.

## Technical requirements
Patients need the patient app on a smartphone with a camera, and a stable internet connection. Consultations are recorded only with the patient's explicit consent.

## Fees
Telehealth follow-ups cost the same as in-person follow-ups and are covered by most insurance plans.
