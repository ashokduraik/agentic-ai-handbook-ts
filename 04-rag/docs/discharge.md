# Inpatient Discharge Process (fictional)

## Discharge time
Discharge orders are written by 11:00 on the day of discharge, and patients should leave the room by 13:00.

## Discharge summary
Every patient receives a discharge summary listing diagnoses, medication changes and follow-up appointments. The summary is also shared to the patient app within 24 hours.

## Follow-up
A nurse calls the patient within 48 hours after discharge for patients with heart failure, COPD or after major surgery.
