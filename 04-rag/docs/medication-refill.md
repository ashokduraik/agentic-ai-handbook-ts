# Prescription Refill Policy (fictional)

## Chronic medication
Refills for chronic medication (for example hypertension or diabetes medication) can be requested through the patient app up to 7 days before the current supply runs out. A refill is approved by the treating physician within 2 working days.

## Controlled medication
Controlled medication (for example opioids or benzodiazepines) cannot be refilled through the app. The patient must attend an in-person appointment.

## Pharmacy pickup
Approved refills can be collected at the outpatient pharmacy, 08:00–22:00, with a national ID or iqama.
