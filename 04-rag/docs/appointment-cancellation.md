# Outpatient Appointment Cancellation Policy (fictional)

## How to cancel
Patients can cancel through the patient app, the call centre (8001234567), or at reception. Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before the appointment.

## Late cancellations and no-shows
A cancellation within 24 hours, or not attending, counts as a no-show. After three no-shows within 12 months, the patient must book through the call centre only and a SAR 100 deposit is required for each new booking.

## Exceptions
Medical emergencies and hospital admissions are not counted as no-shows when documented.
