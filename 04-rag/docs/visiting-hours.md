# Visiting Hours Policy (Al Noor General Hospital — fictional)

## General wards
Visiting hours on general wards are 10:00–13:00 and 16:00–21:00 daily. A maximum of two visitors per patient may be at the bedside at one time.

## Intensive Care Unit (ICU)
ICU visiting is limited to immediate family, 11:00–12:00 and 18:00–19:00. Visitors must perform hand hygiene and wear a gown. Children under 12 may not visit the ICU unless approved by the charge nurse.

## Pediatric ward
One parent or legal guardian may stay with the child 24 hours a day. Siblings may visit during general visiting hours.
